Preserves cumulative256. No starport shrinking, scenery density reduction,

IMPLEMENTED
- Finite-coordinate raster min/max/floor/ceil helpers replace repeated libm
  calls per triangle row. Negative coordinates receive exact floor correction.
- Textured 2x2 samples compute visible depth ownership once and reuse that mask
  for writes, rather than testing all four depths twice.
- Whole-screen-plane triangle rejection occurs AFTER sorting with a2px guard.
  Keeping the original queue is important for coplanar equal-depth ownership.
- Cloud-sprite source colour/alpha products are computed once per sprite in
  bounded2KiB stack tables instead of multiplied for every covered pixel.
- Both build scripts use O3, link-time optimization and fno-math-errno. Source
  does not depend on math errno. No fast-math or unsafe reassociation enabled.
  Windows GCC15.2 build tested; Linux recipe mirrored, not executed.
