ELITE: NEXT 2.5.259 - nearby two-PSP free flight

PLAYER SETUP
Use the same259 EBOOT on both PSPs. Turn on both WLAN switches, keep consoles
nearby, and use the same ad-hoc channel (Auto on both is fine to try first).
On FLY, go down to HOST on one PSP. Its friend chooses JOIN, then X on the
discovered flight. Host presses X to fly/resume; guest enters flight on join.
No router or external matchmaking account is used on physical PSPs.
R/L accelerate/brake, R+Up boosts, Triangle faces friend, Select opens session.
Circle on the session screen ends it. It is not Circle during ordinary flight.

FIRST SCOPE
Two pilots in the host's current solar system, retaining their own ship,
commander name and paint. Planets, three hubs, rifts, debris and traffic come
from the host. Friend ships and guest traffic interpolate between updates.
This is reversible free flight, not full cooperative progression. Combat,
jobs, shops, landing, hyperjumps and saves are blocked during the session.
Both original commander states (including unsaved pre-session progress)
return on exit, disconnection, timeout or sleep. No save schema change.
Local ad-hoc sessions are not authenticated: use with nearby trusted friends.
PSP models without a WLAN radio cannot use this mode.
