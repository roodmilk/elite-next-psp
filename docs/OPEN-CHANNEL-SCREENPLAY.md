# THE OPEN CHANNEL — Feature Screenplay & Campaign Design

**Status:** Canonical story authority for ELITE: NEXT 2.5.20+  
**Form:** Feature-length interactive screenplay (approx. 110–130 script pages / ~10 hours critical path)  
**Playable binding:** `src/saga.h` (six-beat PSP briefs) · `docs/OPEN-CHANNEL-CAMPAIGN.md` (summary bible)  
**Rule:** This document expands dialogue, branches, missions and character arcs. GalNet, novels and tourist-beacon text are **reference only** — never reproduced.

---

## 0. Logline

A Guild coordinator and an independent pilot chase a missing surveyor through the Old Worlds and discover that a navigation corporation has been selling “safe lanes” by amplifying the migration songs of living spaceborne animals — and that something nonhuman has begun to answer the silence left behind.

---

## 1. Theme

**Routes are power.** Whoever writes the chart writes who lives.  
**Observation is not ownership.** Scanning is an ethic, not a loot verb.  
**Institutions fail when their records cannot be challenged.** GalCop’s seal once meant the lane belonged to everyone; near the end it meant nobody knew who was responsible.  
**Dependence is not consent.** Reliable private routes can still be a cage.

Tone: working pilots, moral ambiguity, quiet dread. Thargoids remain rare and unknowable — traces, one remote contact, one answering echo. No disposable alien parade. Mycoid is history, never a craftable weapon.

## 3. Character bible

### KEI AVEN — Explorers Guild coordinator
- **Age band:** mid-30s. **Look:** short dark hair, Guild pin worn crooked from habit.
- **Speech:** precise clauses; panic → over-explanation; never swears on open channel.
- **Want:** bring Ryn home. **Need:** accept that care ≠ control. **Wound:** lost a trainee to a “safe” chart. **Lie:** “If I brief hard enough, nobody dies.”
- **Samples:** “Ryn never missed a call.” / “Start with ‘I’m alive.’” / “Asking you to find her also gave you a home.”
- **Arc:** controller → collaborator → someone who can leave a berth warm without owning it.

### RYN VALE — missing surveyor
- **Age band:** early 30s. **Look:** sun-faded flight suit, recorder always clipped.
- **Speech:** wry, observational, cuts her own jokes short when scared.
- **Want:** keep the migration corridor unowned. **Need:** trust a crew with danger. **Wound:** watched a convoy die on a sold “clear lane.” **Lie:** “If I vanish, everyone else is safer.”
- **Samples:** “I’m alive. The clever part can wait.” / “Three powers want one map. None should own it.” / “It answered the empty space we left.”

### VENN CALDER — Lave traffic / ex-GalCop archivist
- **Speech:** procedure as grief management; softens only for berth assignments.
- **Want:** lanes that answer to records. **Need:** forgive the seal that failed him.
- **Samples:** “Berth six is held.” / “The old seal meant the lane belonged to everyone.”

### IONA MARR — local law
- **Speech:** short sentences; allergic to speeches; respects timestamps.
- **Want:** evidence chains that survive politics. **Need:** accept imperfect allies.
- **Samples:** “A badge is not evidence. Mine included.” / “Someone must hold the keys, and accept inspection.”

### ORU SEN — xenobiologist
- **Speech:** patient, naming carefully; hates premature labels.
- **Want:** observation without extraction. **Need:** publish without becoming a prophet.
- **Samples:** “Silence is part of the message.” / “Then our first useful act was making room.”

### TAMSIN REED — convoy captain
- **Speech:** freighter-practical; measures distance in fuel and crew hours.
- **Want:** crews that come home. **Need:** believe a public chart can be honest again.

### NADI QUILL & PIP — salvage + drone
- **Nadi:** grease-humour, protective of Pip. **Pip:** literal, timestamps everything.
- **Samples (Pip):** “Signal structure: nonhuman. Confidence: troubling.”

### SABLE ROOK — pirate negotiator
- **Speech:** courteous threat; bargains explicit; never “reformed.”
- **Want:** profit from truth as much as from silence. **Need:** none — she’s a foil who keeps her word.
- **Samples:** “Meridian pays well for routes nobody questions.” / “The Empire never calls a debt a chain.”

### DIRECTOR CASSIAN VOSS — Pale Meridian
- **Speech:** corporate sincerity; believes reliability is morality.
- **Want:** own the predictable lane. **Need:** (if reached) admit dependence ≠ consent.
- **Rule:** can stand down, evacuate, or flee — never a mandatory kill.

### Supporting
- **Mara** — agricultural dock; sealed the receiver.  
- **Envoy Hale (Federation)** — logistics + surveillance fine print.  
- **Patron Lysara (Empire)** — courtesy as long debt.  
- **Delegate Mirek (Alliance)** — witnesses and public minutes.

## 6. PROLOGUE — Chapter 01: First Flight

**INT. LAVE SYSTEM HUB — BERTH SIX — DAYCYCLE**

Traffic murmur. A cheap ship that still smells like someone else’s coffee.

**VENN**  
Berth six is held. Don’t make me hold it twice.

**KEI**  
Commander. I’m Kei Aven — Explorers Guild. I need a pilot who can come back.

**YOU**  
What’s the job?

**KEI**  
Ryn Vale. Surveyor. She missed three scheduled calls. She misses meals. Once she missed a moon. She does not miss calls.

**VENN**  
*(without looking up)*  
And Lave will still be here when you decide whether that matters.

**KEI**  
I will not send you into a war. Not today. Launch. Clear the station. Dock again. Prove the ship and the pilot are the same person.

**YOU**  
And after that?

**KEI**  
After that we go looking. Carefully.

### Mission
- Accept locked six-beat brief at Lave Hub.  
- Launch, fly ≥600 m, return and dock (guided or manual).  
- Claim 100 U + harbour badge. Opens Open Channel.  
- **Recovery:** training death restores cargo/fuel at Lave; emergency tow ≠ completion.

### Prologue dialogue (locked beats — playable)

1. KEI: “Ryn is missing. Help me find her.” / “She missed three calls. That is not like her.”  
2. “I will not send you into combat yet.” / “The catch is simple: come back alive.”  
3. “Ryn flew a ship like this one.” / “Borrow it. Learn its habits. Bring it home.”  
4. “Analog or D-pad — pick what feels true.” / “Select opens the deck when you need air.”  
5. “Launch, clear the station, then dock again.” / “That proves you can carry the next job.”  
6. “First flight: launch, fly, return to Lave Hub.” / “Accept when you are ready to begin.”
