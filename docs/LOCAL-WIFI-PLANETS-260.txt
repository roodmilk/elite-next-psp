ELITE: NEXT 2.5.260 - Two-player planetary co-exploration

Both PSPs need this EBOOT and their WLAN switches on. Start Host/Join from
FLY, then Select opens the session screen. Up/Down chooses a planet; X
starts its existing cloud-covered first-person arrival. Each pilot lands
independently. To meet, choose the same planet. Surface/scenery seeds are
the host system's; different planets do not display each other's entities.

On foot: D-pad walks/strafes, analogue looks, hold R runs, tap R jumps.
Tap Square cycles visible targets; hold Square opens the targeting computer.
Circle is the existing handheld scanner/tool selector. Triangle opens a
just-scanned Codex record or boards your own nearby ship. X enters your own
nearby Roamer or a planetary site scene. Start opens the planetary map.
In the Roamer, R accelerates, L brakes, Circle drifts, X boosts; Triangle
parks/exits when stopped. Aboard your ship, X steps out and R launches.
Arrival/seat/departure cinematics retain button blocking/release guards.
Select -> Circle ends the session and restores the original commander.

The two existing large traffic berths become PILOT 1/PILOT 2 in sessions;
ambient descending ships are suppressed there, not drawn over player hulls.
Host uses the left bay, guest the right. The open garage allocates separate
Roamers forty metres apart near its front. Single-player layout/traffic is
unchanged. Existing landing/disembark physics now use an ownership-aware
landing pad. Remote parked ships and Roamers have matching visible collision
bounds; no entire empty landing pad becomes an invisible obstacle.

Simple code-native block suits have a helmet/visor, torso, arms, moving legs
and ground shadow, tinted to the pilot's ship paint. Remote Roamers and parked
ships are drawn in the same terrain depth pass, not through hills/buildings.
Pilot/vehicle movement is interpolated. Body/surface/vehicle/transition state
is sequenced and validated; coordinate-space changes snap the replica before
interpolation, preventing orbit-to-ground streaks and wrong-planet ghosts.
Host wildlife snapshots reach guests sharing its planet; guest physics does
not independently simulate those animals. Personal scan flags are retained
within the session rather than copied from the host. Other planets use their
own local wildlife until the host visits them; shared persistent simulation
across several planets is not implemented.

Still a reversible exploration sandbox, NOT persistent cooperative jobs,
combat, economy or hyperspace. Site interactions/scans are session-local and
are discarded on exit. No save format changed. Early commanders can land in
the session without permanently granting campaign landing technology.
Complete original Game is restored on leave, disconnect, timeout or sleep.
Transient ownership/replica/peer collision fields are not serialized.

Protocol 260 uses 1144-byte datagrams (<1400), still bounded nonblocking PDP
traffic: two host chunks and at most eight received packets per frame.
One additional wildlife chunk per world burst; no assets streamed by Wi-Fi.
Protected Game 85064 bytes; static ELF text+data+BSS 9950196 bytes.
MEMSIZE=0, eight-MiB heap and one-MiB main stack retained. Not authenticated;
both peers must run matching PSP builds. No public internet service.
