See server/README.txt for startup and PSP configuration. PC address at setup:
192.168.1.105, UDP25926. Two pilots maximum; eight client slots reserved for later.
No public server, port forwarding or automatic Windows firewall changes.

Title Square: online options. Triangle: enable/disable. Load/start commander:
auto-connect while enabled. FLY / NEARBY PLAYERS: U/D select, X track, Circle back.
Directory lists all connected pilots and sorts your own system first.
Space ships only in same system; planet avatars/Roamers only on same planet.
Docked pilots target the station; planet-bound pilots target their planet.
Different systems use ordinary route/fuel/range rules, not free teleportation.
Saves and purchases remain personal; docked manual saving remains available.

This presence prototype does not synchronize NPC combat, trades, bounty claims,
missions or an MMO economy. Private development protocol is not encrypted or
authenticated for public Internet use. NAT/firewall traversal, larger populations
and physical PSP Wi-Fi/FPS remain unverified. Direct ad-hoc Host/Join retained.
