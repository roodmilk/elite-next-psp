CAUSE
Lave I's authored observatory explicitly bypassed surface_landmark_v2 and its
ground foundation. Its basement started8m below only the centre terrain
height. The hill beneath the rest of the footprint rose above its lower walls.
Generic foundations sampled only centre/four outer corners, missing interior
mesh vertices/diagonals and differing coarse horizon heights.

FIX
surface-landmarks.h now finds the maximum of each clipped ground triangle
over the expanded site rectangle: clipped corners and diagonal endpoints
cover the extrema. Checks both Lave160/960m meshes or generic40/280m meshes.
Sixteen exact-tagged entries cache levels by system/body/type/seed/position/
dimensions/site kind; about704 bytes, no per-frame heap allocation.
Grade is at least0.6m above intersecting rendered terrain. The existing
biome-coloured earth skirt/thin engineered footing remains; no universal grey
full-height plinth restored. Lave I observatory now uses that footing, with
its existing8m basement offset compensated so the bottom is above the grade.
Structure visibility, mounted signs and POI target markers use the corrected
origin rather than the old centre height. X/Z identities, interactions,
collision reservations, caves, controls, saves and player music unchanged.
Open solid-world crystal grotto interiors deliberately retain ground-level
placement instead of being raised to an inaccessible platform.
