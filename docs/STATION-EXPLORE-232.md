# Station Explore shell — 2.5.232

The station walkabout now follows the selected cinematic split-screen concept while preserving the existing gameplay and 340x168 authored room area.

## Browse state

- `HERE` is always the first entry in the `EXPLORE` rail.
- Selecting `HERE` restores the room title and complete arrival description.
- `HERE` focuses the complete room frame; people, doors, props and services keep their own precise hotspot glow.
- Hovering still does not replace committed description or dialogue text. `X` is required to inspect or act.
- Credits remain visible at the top-right and all controls remain on the bottom edge.

## Procedural compatibility

`HERE` is generated by the shared hotspot builder rather than authored per room. It therefore applies to all seven room types and every station identity without additional content data. Room descriptions continue to come from the existing room-content layer, so future station-specific prose can replace generic text without changing the interface.

## Verification

The station reading suite checks that `HERE` is first, covers the exact 340x168 scene, and restores the current room's arrival prose after another hotspot has been inspected.
